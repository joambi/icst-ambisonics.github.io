**Audience:** Visitors, Students, Composers.

## Why this guide
Spatial listening can feel abstract at first. This page provides a simple method for hearing structure, movement, and depth more clearly.

## Listening method
1. Orientation: identify the room and the loudspeaker impression.
2. Focus: track one sonic object over time.
3. Contrast: compare foreground and background layers.
4. Reflection: note what changed your perception.

## During the session
- Keep one short note per piece.
- Mark one timestamp with the strongest spatial effect.
- Avoid over-analyzing in the first pass.

## After the session
- Compare notes with another listener.
- Reconnect findings to compositional techniques.
