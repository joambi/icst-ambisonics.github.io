Institute for Computer Music and Sound Technology / (ICST) Zurich University of the Arts

---

The ICST Composition Studio at ZHdK is a higher-order Ambisonics production environment for electroacoustic music and sound art. This section documents the studio, its equipment, events, and the work of artists in residence.
