Composing in Ambisonics means treating space not merely as a distribution system, but as a **form-building parameter**. Sources can approach, separate, rise, cluster, or act as independent voices within a shared sound field.

This area is not meant as a linear textbook, but as a **compositional workspace in text form**. It connects historical context, listening practice, compositional questions, and technical toolchains.

## What You Will Find Here

- **Analytical Listening**: an entry point into hearing spatial music consciously
- **History of FOA and HOA**: historical lines, studios, concert spaces, archives, and tools
- **10 Questions in 3D Space**: a practical question set for planning and reflecting on a piece
- **Spatial Counterpoint**: spatial voice-leading, case studies, and ICST/REAPER workflows
- **Tools and Software**: an overview of key Ambisonics toolchains
