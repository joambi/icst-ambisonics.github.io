Working with Ambisonics means working within a historical field, not just with a technique. For composers, the distinction between **FOA** and **HOA** is especially important: FOA is the historical basis and remains a robust production format; HOA is the later systematic expansion toward higher spatial resolution and more flexible rendering.

### Key Points

- **FOA** is the historical root and remains practical for teaching, sketching, production, and distribution.
- **HOA** emerges as a more systematic modern field from around 2000 onward and marks a clear increase in spatial precision.
- **Compositional consequence**: not every work needs HOA, but many current immersive forms become more flexible and transferable through it.

### Why This Matters Practically

For compositional practice, this history is not just background. It explains why Ambisonics work today almost always mediates between three levels: **format and order** (FOA or HOA), **playback context** (dome, studio, binaural, mobile setup), and **toolchain** (DAW, encoder, decoder, monitoring, automation).

That leads directly to the next set of questions: how many spatial layers does a piece need, how should material move, how precise should localisation be, and how can a work be documented so that it is not tied to one loudspeaker arrangement only?
