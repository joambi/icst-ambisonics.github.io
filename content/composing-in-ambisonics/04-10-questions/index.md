These questions are meant as a **compositional worksheet**. They are useful both for analysing existing works and for planning your own pieces, studio sessions, and documentation strategies.

### 1. Ontology of the Piece

What kind of world does the piece construct in space? Are you working with recognisable places, abstract formations, individual sound objects, or broader textures and fields?

### 2. Role of Space

What primarily carries the form: spatial gesture or spectral-temporal development? Could the piece still work in stereo, or would the form collapse?

### 3. Spatial Layers

How many distinguishable spatial layers are there? Which zones matter most: front, back, above, below, near, or far?

### 4. Object Count and Density

How full is the space at any given moment? How many active objects can remain perceptually distinct?

### 5. Trajectories and Gesture Types

Which types of movement define the piece: circles, lines, vertical motion, explosions, swarms, or more gestural motions of approach and escape?

### 6. Localisation vs. Diffuseness

When should sounds be sharply localised, and when should they become diffuse or enveloping?

### 7. Target Listening Scenario

For which playback situation are you composing: dome, multichannel loudspeaker array, binaural, or multiple formats?

### 8. Audience and Listening Experience

How spatially trained is the assumed audience? Should contrasts be obvious and legible, or can they remain subtle?

### 9. Real-Space Reference and Archetypes

How does the piece relate to real spaces and spatial metaphors? Is it mimetic, transformed, or physically impossible?

### 10. Work Identity and Documentation

What actually counts as the work: the HOA master, a specific decoded version, or a binaural release? What needs to be documented so the work remains reconstructible?
