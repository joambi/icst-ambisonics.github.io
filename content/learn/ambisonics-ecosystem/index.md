The ICST Ambisonics EcoSystem brings together all the tools, formats and workflows used at the Institute for Computer Music and Sound Technology (ICST, ZHdK) to compose, produce and perform spatial sound. The tools are free and open source. Students, visiting artists and the development team all work with the same infrastructure.

## Two tools, two ways of working

### ICST Ambisonics Plugins (REAPER)

Encoder, decoder, multi-decoder, monitor and animator for Higher-Order Ambisonics up to 7th order, with up to 64 sources and 64 loudspeakers. Time-based, intuitive, with OSC control.

**Recommended for:** fixed media, film, multimedia, studio production.

### ICST Ambisonics Tools (Max/MSP)

Externals for live spatialisation, algorithmic sound design and OSC integration. Modular and flexible, but requires Max knowledge.

**Recommended for:** live electronics, special performances.

## Common foundation

- **Format:** AmbiX (SN3D/ACN), compatible with the wider ecosystem
- **Studio:** ICST Composition Studio with 18 loudspeakers (Ambisonics Full)
- **Learning:** tutorials, best practices, blog, Acousmatic Listening Sessions (ICST Ascolta)

## The wider ecosystem

The ICST tools can be combined with other Ambisonics tools such as the IEM Plugin Suite, Ambisonic Toolkit (ATK), Envelop for Live or SPAT. The common denominator is the AmbiX format.
