## Encoder and decoder shortcuts

**Actions available in Encoder and Decoder:**

- **Zoom In:** _Right Mouse Button Click / MouseWheel_
- **Zoom Out**: _Shift + Right Mouse Button Click_ / _MouseWheel_
- **Reset Zoom:** _Home-Button (OSX: Fn + Left Arrow)_
- **Select Multiple Points:** _Shift + Click Multiple Points or Select rectangular Region using Drag-Drop_

**Actions available in Multi-Channel-Encoder only:**

- **Select Multiple Groups:** _Shift + Click Multiple Group Points_
- **Move Group Point(s) only:** _Shift + Drag-Drop Selected Group Point(s)_
- **Stretch Group:** _Alt -> Stretchsymbol -> Mouse Up/down to increase/decrease stretch_
- **Rotate Point(s) around Group Point(s):** _Alt -> Drag-Drop Rotate-Around-Group-Symbol_
- **Rotate Group(s) around Origin:** _Alt -> Drag-Drop Rotate-Around-Origin-Symbol_
  

**Mute & Solo** (direct in the Radar)

- shift + control & 'm' [Mute]
- shift + control & 's' [Solo]
