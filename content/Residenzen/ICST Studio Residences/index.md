# Artist Residencies at the ICST Composition Studio

At the ICST Composition Studio, international artists create 3D audio works using Ambisonics technology. The programme enables creative work in spatial sound design and electroacoustic composition, with direct access to the ICST team and tools.

Residents typically stay for one to four weeks — in a single block or spread across multiple sessions. No prior Ambisonics experience is required; the ICST team provides full onboarding on the plugins and workflows.
