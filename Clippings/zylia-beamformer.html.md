## ZYLIA 3D Audio Suite

## ZYLIA Beamformer

This powerful plugin revolutionizes spatial audio workflows by delivering real-time sound source separation, interactive visualizations, and highly customizable virtual microphone setups — all directly in your Digital Audio Workstation (DAW).  

## Key Features

#### Advanced Beamforming and Source Separation

- Easily configure and manage virtual microphones with customizable polar patterns.
- Incorporates traditional microphone polar patterns such as Figure-of-8 and Cardioid.
- Utilize real-time separation modes to isolate audio sources with exceptional accuracy.

#### Interactive Visualization and Source Localization

- Real-time energy maps visually display the location of sound sources.
- Automatic sound source localization.

#### Comprehensive DAW Integration

- Integrates with leading DAWs, including Reaper, Pro Tools, and Nuendo.
- Available as VST, AU, and AAX plugins, ensuring broad compatibility.

#### Ambisonics Format Support

- Designed explicitly for processing Ambisonics signals up to 7th order.
- Fully compatible with Ambix formats (ACN, SN3D).

## Abstandsniveaueinstellung

##### Durch die Erhöhung des Trennniveaus wendet ZYLIA Beamformer zusätzliche Verarbeitung an, um das Nahmikrofonverhalten zu simulieren.

  

- **Hoher Trennungspegel** – Weiter reduziert Umgebungsgeräusche und Hintergrundgeräusche, was die Isolation zwischen den Quellen verbessert.
- **Blind Source Separation Algorithm** – Diese fortschrittliche nichtlineare Signalverarbeitungstechnik verbessert die Klangtrennung, kann aber die Klangfarbe des aufgenommenen Audios geringfügig verändern.
