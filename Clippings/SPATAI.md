**S** **PATAI** is a utility software that offers **Large Language Model AI assistance to move sound objects** in a vitual 3D acoustic space.

It translates in real time the movement of multiple objects in cartiesian or polar coordinates and sends this movement data to other 3D audio spatialization software, which renders or encodes the audio present at its input channels according to these received movement instructions.

**SpatAI** creates complex motion of multiple objects with AI.

It then communicates with third-party applications via OSC or MIDI to send each movement on a time line.

**SpatAI** does not encode ambisonic audio or render digital audio in any format, it creates the motion data compatible for other third party software.

**prompt -> AI based motion design**

**up to 32 objects with coordinated movement**

**supports Cartesian and Polar coordinate systems**

**real-time macro controls acting on LLM motion script.**

**transport / playback controls for defined region.**

**OSC / MIDI output**

**currently supported destinations: [IEM](https://plugins.iem.at/), [Holophonix](https://holophonix.xyz/), [Ircam SPAT,](https://forum.ircam.fr/projects/detail/spat/) [Ircam Panoramix](https://forum.ircam.fr/projects/detail/panoramix/), [Yamaha AFC](https://usa.yamaha.com/products/proaudio/afc/index.html).**
