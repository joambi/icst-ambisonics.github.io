## ⚠️ Hauptschwächen & was verbessert werden kann

### 🔥 1. Unklare Zielgruppenansprache

Aktuell wirkt die Startseite wie ein Blog-Feed ohne **klaren Wertversprechen-Abschnitt** („Was ist Ambisonics? Warum diese Seite?“).

→ **Empfehlung:**  
Füge auf der Startseite direkt oben eine **Kurz-Einführung** für zwei Zielgruppen:

- Für Einsteiger:innen (Was ist Ambisonics? Vorteile?)
- Für Fortgeschrittene (Tools, Plugins, Downloads, Forschung)

## ICST Ambisonics Philosophy

*Field-Based Spatial Composition and Decoding*

---

## 1\. Why Another Ambisonics Platform?

Ambisonics is often presented as a neutral encoding format — mathematically elegant, flexible, and format-independent.

At the **Institute for Computer Music and Sound Technology**, Ambisonics is not treated as a neutral container.

It is treated as:

> A compositional field model.

This distinction defines our philosophy.

---

## 2\. From Channel-Based to Field-Based Thinking

Conventional spatial production paradigms (stereo, 5.1, Atmos) are channel-based systems.  
They define loudspeaker targets.

Ambisonics, however, describes a continuous spatial sound field using spherical harmonics.

At ICST, we adopt a strict field-based perspective:

- The B-format is the primary artistic representation.
- Loudspeaker arrays are rendering devices — not compositional targets.
- Decoding is interpretation, not translation.

This leads to fundamentally different design decisions.

---

## 3\. The Problem with “Neutral” Decoding

Standard decoding approaches (e.g. ALLRAD-style vector-based methods) optimize geometric energy distribution.

They are mathematically consistent.

However, studio practice revealed recurring perceptual phenomena:

- Depth blurring in complex scenes
- Frequency-dependent spatial diffusion
- Reduced vertical precision in practical arrays
- Instability when translating between layouts

These observations motivated a re-evaluation of decoding assumptions.

---

## 4\. The ICST Multi-Band Field Approach

The ICST Ambisonics system introduces a perceptually informed strategy:

### Core Principles

1. **Field coherence over speaker precision**
2. **Frequency-dependent spatial structuring**
3. **Layered decoding architectures**
4. **Array-aware rendering**
5. **Studio-validated perceptual testing**

Instead of a single global decoding matrix, the ICST MultiDecoder architecture allows:

- Independent decoding layers (e.g., height / mid / low field)
- Frequency-dependent weighting
- Controlled spatial focus vs. diffusion
- Adaptation to non-symmetric arrays

This does not abandon Ambisonics theory.  
It extends it toward perceptual practice.

---

## 5\. Ambisonics as Composition, Not Only Technology

At ICST, Ambisonics is not primarily an engineering problem.

It is a compositional space.

The decoder becomes:

- A spatial instrument
- A timbral shaping tool
- A depth-sculpting mechanism
- A field articulation device

This perspective emerges from real studio production and performance contexts, not purely theoretical models.

---

## 6\. Research Embedded in Studio Reality

All ICST Ambisonics tools are developed and tested in real production environments at the:

- Zurich University of the Arts
- ICST 3D Composition Studio

Development is informed by:

- Composer feedback
- Live performance constraints
- Translation between arrays
- Psychoacoustic listening sessions
- Empirical comparison of decoding strategies

The system evolves through practice.

---

## 7\. Beyond Plugin Thinking

The ICST Ambisonics Decoder is not a standalone plugin.

It is part of an integrated system:

Encoding → Spatial Structuring → Multi-Layer Decoding → Array Rendering

This systemic view distinguishes our work from isolated plugin implementations.

---

## 8\. A Critical Position

We do not consider Ambisonics a solved technology.

Questions we actively investigate:

- How does decoding influence perceived depth?
- Can frequency-layered decoding enhance spatial clarity?
- What are the aesthetic implications of upmixing historical works?
- How stable is Higher-Order Ambisonics across real-world arrays?
- Is mathematical optimality equivalent to perceptual optimality?

These questions guide our development.

---

## 9\. Ambisonics as Living Practice

Ambisonics at ICST is:

- Research-driven
- Composition-centered
- Studio-validated
- System-oriented
- Perceptually informed

It is not merely about reproducing space.

It is about shaping space.
