## What you will learn in this course

Understand how 3D audio differs from stereo and surround sound

Get to know Ambisonics as a scene-based audio format

Learn how to work with First-Order and Higher-Order Ambisonics

Explore the workflow using REAPER and IEM plugins

Understand how binaural audio works and why headphones matter

## What skills and tools you need for this course

This course is designed for beginners — no prior experience with 3D audio or Ambisonics is required. You should know what a DAW (Digital Audio Workstation) is, and how to work with audio tracks. What you really need is curiosity for spatial sound: If you're interested in how sound moves in space — you're in the right place! To experience binaural audio examples properly, good stereo headphones are essential.
