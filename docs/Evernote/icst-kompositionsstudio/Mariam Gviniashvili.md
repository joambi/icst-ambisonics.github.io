Institute for Computer Music and Sound Technology / (ICST) Zurich University of the Arts

* * *

**Mariam Gviniashvili** will be our guest at ICST composition studio from 13.06.2022 - 19.06.2022. 
She was awarded PRIX CIME and received this residency as a prize for it. Mariam is working on a new audiovisual piece in multichannel format to be premiered in Berlin, at the Heroines of Sound festival in July.
![[./_resources/Mariam_Gviniashvili.resources/IMG_6131.jpg]]
We wish her a lot of inspiration power and success!
