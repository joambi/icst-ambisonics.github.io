## Project Proposal

In response to this year's residency theme, "Spatial Sound Synthesis", I propose to create an electro-acoustic musical composition. This composition will have two main objectives: 

* First, it will deal with synthetic sounds by extracting spectral elements from recordings and then recomposing and recontextualising them in the creative process. 
* Secondly, she will explore the concept of space as a form that can be moulded and shaped by establishing both sonic qualities and causal relationships between the various sound components integrated into the composition. 

With the experience I have gained working with Ambisonics, I intend to engage with the intersection of sound synthesis and sound spatialisation as described in the residency call. In line with the aforementioned aims, I would like to experiment with the use of spectrally extracted sounds from various recordings. In this composition I would like to explore the concept of space as form through the recomposition and recontextualisation of these sounds. This exploration involves the creation of spatial forms and the establishment of causal and structural spatial connections between the materials. 
Specifically, "causal relationship" means that I experiment with how sound materials interact with space. For example, a loud gesture can trigger a change in spatial perception, where the response to the gesture is not perceived as another sound localised or diffused in space, but as a sound that embodies the space itself. 
In terms of space and form, for example, one could experiment with the timbre of continuous textures by emphasising or altering different frequency bands depending on where the texture is located in space. For example, this manipulation could result in a texture that moves in cycles, emphasising high frequencies when it is in the frontal perspective and low frequencies when it is in the background. Another example is the structural spatial development of sounds that gradually start from a narrow localisation and gradually expand to fill the entire sphere. This creates the impression of a living organism that develops and expands over time. 
The work plan for this project is that I will spend three weeks collecting materials and making an initial compositional draft or sketch before coming to the residency. I will then spend three weeks in the Ambisonic studio to realise the project using the Ambisonic system. 
To summarise, this project is an exciting journey into the realm of Spatial Sound Synthesis. It builds on my evolving compositional direction and the valuable experience I have gained from exploring Ambisonic. Through the fusion of sound synthesis and sound spatialisation, this project aims to breathe life into the concept of space as form. Through experimentation, I endeavour to create a composition that pushes the boundaries of my musical language and offers the listener a new, immersive listening experience. With careful planning and lots of time in the studio, I look forward to realising this vision during the residency.
