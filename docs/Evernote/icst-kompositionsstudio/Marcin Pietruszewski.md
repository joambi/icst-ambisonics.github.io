Institute for Computer Music and Sound Technology / (ICST) Zurich University of the Arts

* * *

Marcin Pietruszewski works as ICST Artist in Residency from **6/27/2022 to 7/17/2022** in the ICST Composition Studio.
![[./_resources/Marcin_Pietruszewski.resources/IMG_0979.JPG]]
Marcin is working on a new 'piece' for multichannel (Ambisonics). He is using his own instrument 'Pulsar Generator (nuPg)' for this. This SuperCollider (SC3) instrument, he will extend with our 'ICST Ambisonics Plugins' for 3D audio.
![[./_resources/Marcin_Pietruszewski.resources/IMG_0981.JPG]]

## The New [Pulsar Generator (nuPg)](https://www.marcinpietruszewski.com/the-new-pulsar-generator) is an interactive program for sound synthesis developed in SuperCollider 3 (SC3) programming language.

You can find more information about Marcin Pietruszewski here: <https://www.marcinpietruszewski.com/>
