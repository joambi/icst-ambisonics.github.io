[Playtime](https://www.helgoboss.org/projects/playtime/) is a VST instrument for the Digital Audio Workstation [REAPER](http://www.reaper.fm/) and works in **REAPER only**. It is designed to make loop-based music production in REAPER easier and much more fun. In particular, it offers a Session View loosely inspired by the one in [Ableton Live](https://www.ableton.com/de/live/). That means it enables you to play loops in a very intuitive way - just by triggering them using your MIDI controller or the graphical user interface.
