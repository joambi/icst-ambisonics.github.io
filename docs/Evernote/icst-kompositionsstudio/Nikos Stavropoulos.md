### Nikos Stavropoulos

He will be working in the composition studio from **18.07.2022 to 1.08.2022**.
Nikos Stavropoulos is working on a new acousmatic Higher Order Ambisonic work, continuing his research in the notion of aural microspace and intimacy in electroacoustic music. In this new work, he is investigating ways of processing HoA source material recorded with the OctoMic. The new work is expected to be premiered in the UK during the fall of 2022.
![[./_resources/Nikos_Stavropoulos.resources/1658512254578.jpg]]
**Nikos Stavropoulos** (Athens, Greece, 1975) is a composer of predominantly acousmatic and mixed music. He read music at the University of Wales (Bangor, Wales, UK), where he studied composition with [Andrew Lewis](https://electrocd.com/en/artiste/lewis_an/andrew-lewis) and completed a doctorate at the University of Sheffield (England, UK) under the supervision of [Adrian Moore](https://electrocd.com/en/artiste/moore_ad/adrian-moore).
