Olga Koksharova is working from 11.03.2022 - 13.03.2022 in the ICST Kompositionsstudio.
She is working on finishing her composition 'Sculpter la Voute. Altération.' which she created in ambisonics using the ICST Ambisonics plugins as Artis in Residency at ICST in July 2021.

![[./_resources/Olga_Koksharova.resources/IMG_0854.JPG]]
Olga Koksharova is rehearsing her new piece 'Sculpter la Voute. Altération.' which she will perform at the Archipel Festival on the 2nd of April 2022 in Geneva.
In 'Sculpter la Voute. Altération.' Olga is working on a hybrid spatialization concept. She created the piece during her ICST Artist in Residency time as Ambisonics B-format 3rd order. This additional session is about combining the B-format with the Acousmonium available in Geneva. The added loudspeakers of the acousmonium will allow her to work on the timbral components of her piece created in Ambisonics.

# _Mycènes Alpha_, by Iannis Xenakis (1978)

is the first pièce entirely composed on the UPIC. This work was specifically conceived to be integrated in the _Polytope de Mycènes_, which was performed between September 2 – 5, 1978, in the ruins of the acropolis of Mycenae in Greece. ([Quelle](http://www.centre-iannis-xenakis.org/en-upic_mycenae))
![[./_resources/Olga_Koksharova.resources/IMG_0846.JPG]]
Olga Koksharova also creates the Same Hybrid Strategy for this work by Y.Xenakis. (Commissioned by Archipel 2022)
Together Ambisonics and the 'Acousmonium' Olga creates a new interpretation.
