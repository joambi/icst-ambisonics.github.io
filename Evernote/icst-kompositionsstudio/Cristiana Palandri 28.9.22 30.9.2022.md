PALANDRI STAGE at ICST:

I created an electronic piece starting from a pictorial scheme/drawing. In the picture, each sound element is represented graphically with a shape, a color, and a position in paper space, which is two-dimensional. I want to transform the 2D position of the graphic elements into a 3D sonic component of the sound space, to find a correlation between the visual and sound elements. I realized a composition that can inspire a sound vision through hearing and, simultaneously, perceive sound as a sculptural element, coming from a drawing, in its diffusion in space.
