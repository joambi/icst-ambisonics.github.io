# ICST Artist in Residency: Pratyay Raha

Date: 03.07.2023  - 27.07.2023
![[./_resources/Pratyay_Raha.resources/IMG_1180.JPG]]

Pratyay Raha is an Indian sound artist and composer working in the field of new media and acoustic ecology. For this residency, he proposes to create a compositional methodology prototype using concepts from critical theory (object oriented ontology) and ecoacoustics. He plans to use field recordings, audio programming, data sonification and ICST Ambisonics setup to make the prototype
